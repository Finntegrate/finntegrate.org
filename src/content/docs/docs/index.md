Finntegrate documentation brings together practical guidance, clear explanations,
and accountable research about life in Finland.

It is designed for three overlapping audiences:

- **People moving to or living in Finland**, who need understandable next steps.
- **Researchers and advocates**, who need traceable evidence and context.
- **Public officials and service providers**, who need to understand experiences and improve services.

This is a living library. Information that affects decisions benefits from
identifying its source, jurisdiction, and currentness. Finntegrate does not
replace advice from an authorised public authority or qualified professional.
